# Contributing to HFOC OS

Discuss a change before investing substantial work, follow `AGENTS.md`, use a
feature branch and Draft PR, preserve RED → GREEN evidence, and do not weaken
tests.

External contributions require a signed
`CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md`. Until that document and its acceptance
process receive professional legal review, external pull requests may be
reviewed and discussed but must not be merged.

Every contribution must disclose copied, generated, vendored, or embedded
third-party material with its source, exact version or commit, checksum, and
license. Do not remove upstream notices or place third-party material under the
HFOC dual-license expression.

A maintainer may apply `cla: signed` only after retaining a signed acceptance
record associated with the contributor identity. The label, a checkbox, a PR
comment, or a DCO sign-off alone is insufficient.
